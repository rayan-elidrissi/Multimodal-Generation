# 🎨 ModulaRt

**Multimodal Creative Coding with Mathematics, Audio & Visuals**

ModulaRt is a sandbox for mathematical expression through audio and visual generation. Built at the intersection of computation, creativity, and code, this project explores how pure mathematical functions can be used to generate synchronized sound and imagery. Inspired by the harmony of signal processing, geometry, and modular art.

---

## ✨ Highlights

- 📐 **Mathematical Audio Synthesis** – Sound generation driven by π, sine functions, and modular logic.
- 🖼️ **Procedural Visuals** – Mathematical patterns rendered in real-time.
- 🔁 **Synchronized Outputs** – Visuals and sounds tied together through shared generative logic.
- 🔬 **Experimental Sandbox** – Built to explore art-as-algorithm ideas and perceptual multimodality.
